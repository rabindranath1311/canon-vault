Field notes from three weeks of drawing harbour charts by lamp, which turns
out to be a legible constraint rather than an affectation.

A lantern gives you roughly a 300mm working circle. Everything outside it is
inferred. That is a limit on how much of a chart you can hold in your head at
once, and it shows up in the drawing: the older harbour charts have a dense,
confident centre and a vague, apologetic edge.

Modern tools removed the lamp and kept the habit. A canvas that scrolls forever
still produces work that is dense in the middle and vague at the edges — we just
stopped having a reason for it.
