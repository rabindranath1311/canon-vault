Images, screenshots, links and quotations, written straight into your vault folder as plain markdown. No server, no account, nothing uploaded — the same files the app and Obsidian open.

Choose the folder your vault lives in

The one holding CONVENTION.md — the same folder you open in the app and in Obsidian. Clips are written straight into it. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing else on your disk is read.

takes one click
  1. Pin the toolbar icon Click the puzzle-piece in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin beside Canon Clip. That icon is the popup.
  2. Clip anything Right-click an image or a selection, press AltShiftC for the page, or AltShiftS to drag out a region.
  3. Open it in the app It is already there — a clip is a markdown file in the folder you just picked.
